4.2Three tests, from the evidence

The organization-design canon favoured defence-heavy work — fraud, commerce data quality — because it is coordinated by standardized outputs.20 The India evidence named QoE and sessions telemetry as a capability; both case-study tracks named QoE or sessions as candidates for graduating domain ownership, one with fraud first. No track named a day-one domain. What they shared was the criterion: how much does the work require people on the other site to say yes, and how often. The three tests below decompose that question, each traced to the numbered principles it comes from; then a legal gate, and a fifth criterion that decides the Phase 3 choice, not the first domain. judgment

Test 1 — US decision-makers per change (principles 1, 4)

Cross-site work items took about 2.5× as long as single-site items at Lucent because more people were needed per item; temporal separation costs more than distance.625 The test: count the US people who must approve a typical change, in engineering and in the business — under the decision rights the unit would be given, not the US-approves-everything arrangement this memo bans. Commerce packaging needs pricing, finance, legal and trust-and-safety on every change. Telemetry ingestion needs a schema owner. QoE, fraud and ads measurement pass with consultation: India decides, the US stakeholders are consulted, and the twelve-hour gap applies to consultation, not approval.

Test 2 — Output standardizability (principles 1, 7)

Only one of Mintzberg’s configurations — the divisionalized form, coordinated by standardization of outputs — is designed to run a unit at arm’s length; adhocracy, coordinated by mutual adjustment, tolerates distance least; DalleMule and Davenport’s defensive-versus-offensive split maps the same distinction onto data work.1920 The test: can the output be written down as a schema, an SLA or a metric definition a consumer accepts without a conversation? Telemetry can. Fraud metrics can. A funnel analysis cannot.

Test 3 — Separability from the warehouse (principles 2, 5)

Warehouse facts are reciprocally interdependent — every domain reads every other domain’s tables — so a boundary drawn through them produces silos before modules; the 2026 data-mesh retrospective’s failure modes (unfunded domains, re-badged IT teams) are how such a boundary ends with a central platform doing domain work anyway.46123 The test: can the unit be owned whole without the warehouse’s shared facts flowing through it? Ingestion sits upstream and passes; the session fact, the most reciprocally-read table in a streaming warehouse, fails, so sessionization stays central; QoE passes only with sessionization excluded, because its consumers read its tables and do not co-edit them.

The legal gate — cross-border personal data

judgment A gate, not a test. Fraud and paid-sharing data means payment-instrument tokens, household graphs and, for EU subscribers, GDPR-scoped personal data processed in India under its own data-protection law; QoE telemetry means device and network identifiers, no payment instruments, no household graphs. Whether either is transferable as scoped cannot be known from a desk, so the legal review of telemetry, QoE, fraud and ads data is a Phase 0 deliverable owned by privacy counsel, feeding the Gate 0 re-decision; the matrix carries a question glyph wherever the answer waits.

The fifth criterion — robustness to a P&L-driven re-homing

Named because it is not one of the three tests, and it decides the Phase 3 choice, not the first domain: if the data org is re-homed under a different executive — which the legacy-media cases say happens every two to three years — how much would the new owner depend on what the site owns?4445 judgment QoE scores worst: a data org under ad-tech has no use for it. Fraud is in the middle. Ads measurement scores best, with its own warning — it is the data a re-homing owner depends on and the first thing that owner reorganises if the site head is not theirs — and it is owned today by ad-tech, so moving it into this org is a precondition, not a given.

The gate — one accountable owner, on one site (principles 3, 14)

A unit that passes the tests is still not transferable unless one team on one site owns it whole — the Vista finding and Amazon’s, from opposite ends.47 If it cannot be owned whole on one site, it does not move.

Judgment — how the scoring resolves the disagreement

Quality of experience wins on separability and legal exposure. It is the smallest outcome and the least robust to re-homing, and playback engineering computes its own QoE today — so the Phase 2 gate requires it to decommission that computation, not attest to a dashboard. Fraud wins on relevance and loses on legal exposure and the dollar latency of band changes. Sessionization stays central: the session fact is the warehouse’s spine, and a domain that includes it fails Test 3 outright. So QoE is the first domain, transferred at G1 on a written gate — with a re-decision at Gate 0, month 3: if privacy counsel clears fraud as scoped and the confirmed contractor concentration favours commerce, Phase 2 becomes fraud and QoE becomes a capability. Otherwise fraud or ads measurement is the Phase 3 candidate, decided at G1+9. If Gate 2 fails, the domain claim is withdrawn at the next annual review, the QoE metrics roadmap continues as a platform product under the same India lead, and the site is a platform site — acceptable as a site, a failure of this memo’s bet, costed in §4.5. Lower risk first.

Why not fraud first — the strongest alternative. Fraud is defence-heavy, output-standardized (chargeback rate, recovery revenue, false-positive rate), stable in definition, and coordinated by standardized outputs — Mintzberg’s only configuration built to run at arm’s length.1920 Scored under the decision-rights table it would have — India sets thresholds within bands finance and trust and safety own — its US decision-makers per change are no denser than QoE’s, and it is the outcome a CFO notices: the outcome a CFO noticed industry-wide in 2023–25. It loses the first slot on one ground only: payment instruments, household graphs and EU-scoped data processed in India may make it non-transferable as scoped, and that is unknowable until month 3 — hence a re-decision at Gate 0 rather than an eighteen-month wait. If counsel clears it and the contractors sit in platform and QoE, the assumed baseline, QoE stays first because that transfer is a change of ownership rather than of hands, and fraud is the Phase 3 candidate. Commerce packaging, funnels, browse and search fail Test 1 for as long as pricing, bundling and content strategy are set in the US. Experimentation splits at the definition schema: the engine is a capability with a change process and moves; analysis, the catalog and the review gate are adhocratic, stakeholder-adjacent, and stay central, as at every case-study company.

Three changes, walked through

The experimentation engine is the hard case because its main customers stay in the US. The line: India owns the runtime — assignment, exposure logging, metrics computation — and the metric-definition schema, with a published change process and service levels; US analysis is a customer that files definition requests and owns the catalog entry and the review gate. That is X-as-a-service in Team Topologies’ sense — the provider evolves the service on its own roadmap, the consumer uses it with minimal collaboration — and the engine has a customer on its own site, because the QoE team supplies guardrail metrics.43 Three quarterly changes, counted in hand-offs:

  1. A new guardrail metric (rebuffer rate, treatment versus control). If the schema expresses it, analysis files a definition request and India serves it within the SLA: one hand-off. If it needs a source join the schema cannot express, the extension carries a committed-quarter answer, prioritised with the US as customer, and the request follows: two hand-offs, neither a review of the other site’s work. judgment
  2. A redefinition of “active user.” Analysis files the change; India schedules the recomputation under the SLA and, as provider, decides the scheduling and mechanics of recomputation under the SLA; the review gate decides validity — which experiments’ readouts are accepted under which definition — a decision India’s schedule must honour, counted as the one hand-off. The backfill hours are charged to the request, not to India’s gate. judgment
  3. A sample-ratio-mismatch check flags an exposure-logging bug in the US morning. It is a P1 that pages India’s on-call at whatever hour, because that is what owning the component means; fix and validation are India’s, the salvage decision is the review gate’s: one hand-off on the incident, one on the decision. judgment

judgment Under a line drawn at “India computes, the US defines”, each of these is four hand-offs and a provider that cannot say no. Tripwire T4 measures this — hand-offs per change above two at the G0+6 reading, or the definition SLA or committed-quarter answer missed two months running — and Gate 1 measures P1 hours-to-resolve across sites, not P1 count, so a page at 02:00 IST is visible to the plan.

4.4What the US org stops doing

judgment Every distributed-ownership failure in the record shares one feature: the sending site kept a hand on the work.47 So the transfer is written as what the US organization ceases to do, because ownership added in India without being removed in the US is diffuse ownership under a new name.

  • US platform engineers stop owning, gatekeeping and carrying on-call for the transferred components — telemetry ingestion and the experimentation engine from G0, data-quality and contracts tooling from G1. They keep sessionization and the semantic layer, warehouse modeling, governance and access, the ML platform until Phase 3, the catalog entries, and architecture as a standard, not a veto. The US engineers who ran the existing freshness and observability checks are in the Phase 2(c) transfer; their seats are in the §4.5 offset scenario. Bird’s finding — distributed components no worse than collocated ones when ownership was clear and tooling shared — depends on the ownership being real.16
  • The US QoE team is dissolved at G1 — announced at G1−3, retention to G1+3, and from G1−3 to G1+3 its engineers work in facilitating mode, India owning and the US pairing, documented as the transfer mechanism and a stated, time-boxed exception to the half-ownership rule. Its people then redeploy to browse, search, funnels or commerce, or take the one liaison seat. Carmel and Agarwal’s first tactic is to reduce intensive collaboration across sites; two teams owning one domain beyond that window is the maximal-collaboration configuration.18
  • No US manager has India directs. Every India engineer reports to an India manager; every India manager to the site head. judgment Remote directs across the twelve-hour gap are banned as a matter of position, not because a study measured them. The one exception is Phase 1: the site head runs the two teams through two tech leads until the engineering managers start (expected G0+3 to G0+5), the eight-direct rule suspended for that period, because a site whose managers sit elsewhere is the leadership vacuum Zinnov describes.41
  • Half-ownership is banned as a category after G0, with two stated, time-boxed exceptions: the QoE facilitating-mode window, and the quarter-4 shared state in tripwire T3. Before G0 — up to six months — the contractors continue to contribute to US-owned work; that is the one extension phase, named. The experimentation line is X-as-a-service, not shared ownership; T4 tests it. A component that cannot be owned whole on one site is not transferred — Nagappan’s diffuse-ownership result and Amazon’s dependencies-are-defects lesson from opposite ends.4743
Where it breaks

Nothing in a US owner’s incentives pushes the hand-off, and “we can’t wait for India” is how shadow teams start. Tripwire T6 — the quarterly duplicate audit run by the metrics-catalog function — exists for exactly this.

4.5The team-size, span and cost arithmetic

The constraints are Larson’s floor of six and ceiling of eight, and McKinsey’s rule that span follows standardization (Foundations §3.6, §3.11): platform teams at seven to eight, domain teams at five to seven, nothing at ten.4950 Put floor and ceiling together and a small site cannot own two domains without a management layer; scaled to 140 — six people times eight domains before any platform exists — that arithmetic is the mesh retrospective’s unfunded-domain failure mode.123

4.6Reporting lines and rewards

The site head is a peer of the US directors on the data executive’s staff, with a global functional title and accountability for the transferred components and domains, not a country manager. The evidence favours a functional head hired first, because the first hires set the centre’s ceiling, over a country manager or a lift of the vendor’s delivery leads; managers who own outcomes are the scarce tier in the centre market, so the head’s first job is closing the two engineering-manager searches.41141 If the search runs past month 6 nothing transfers (T1); the QoE analytics lead is in seat by G1; one planning calendar; an interim successor named at G1 from the engineering managers and confirmed at Gate 3, so the site is never one resignation from a vacuum.33
