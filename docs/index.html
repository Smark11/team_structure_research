1 The Recommendation

What the India site should own

One answer, defended in two minutes, with the full case underneath.

5,085 words · 22 minSources accessed 2026-08-29

The recommendation

Charter the India site as the accountable owner of three whole components of the data platform and of one whole domain — sessions and quality of experience — from the start. Write a dated graduation for a second domain. Keep commerce, experimentation analysis, browse, search and funnels anchored in the US.

A platform-plus-one-domain hybrid, not a graduating extension. What follows is the two-minute version; every section below it drills into the full defense.

In two minutes

The evidence rules out both pure options and the pure extension. No documented case exists of a global consumer domain handed to a new India centre on day one and succeeding; the India engineering organizations that did own a domain end-to-end — Hotstar, Google Pay — were India-first products with a local P&L, and Hotstar's technology team drained to a competitor the moment the rights economics turned.123 Pure platform ownership is the modal charter and it scales — JPMorgan runs 55,000 people on it — but it is also the modal drift into a cost centre that gets sold or cut when no product outcome depends on it.456 And the graduating extension has a base rate: roughly 27% of India centres reach portfolio ownership within five years, a number that comes from the firm that sells centre set-ups and should be read as a ceiling.7

Why a hybrid and not "a bit of each"

A hybrid with the choices made is a charter. A hybrid decided team by team is the half-ownership configuration every source says fails, because each US owner picks "extension" for their own team. judgment

The evidence supports one shape. Every remote engineering site in the eight case studies holds a capability charter — Netflix Warsaw, Prime Video Bengaluru, WBD's India hub, Sky's platform organization for Peacock, Spotify Boston.89101112 The distributed-work research says distance is survivable for separable work with clear ownership and shared tooling, and fatal for coupled work with diffuse ownership: Microsoft's Vista data showed a negligible quality penalty for distributed teams once ownership was clear, while cross-site work items at Lucent took about 2.5 times as long because more people were needed to close each one.1314 Target's operating rule after fifteen years — "ownership of outcomes rests with where the center of gravity for the capability sits" — is what a working hybrid sounds like.15

Why one domain, and why this one. A platform-only site has no customer of its own and nothing the US cannot ship without. Sessions and quality of experience is the domain that passes the three tests the research converges on — few US decision-makers per change, standardizable outputs, separability from the warehouse's cross-domain dependencies — and it is the domain three of the five research tracks independently named.1617 judgment Fraud and paid sharing is the second candidate: defensive, output-standardizable data work, but with US trust-and-safety, legal and finance stakeholders and decisions denominated in dollars — hence a dated graduation with criteria, not a day-one transfer.18

What does not move. Experimentation analysis, the metrics catalog and the experiment review gate stay one central function, as at Google, Spotify, Netflix and Disney; the experimentation platform — assignment, exposure logging, metrics computation — is a capability India can own.19202122 Commerce packaging, conversion funnels, browse and search stay US-anchored while pricing, bundling and content strategy are set there. judgment

What the US stops doing. This is the part that fails in practice. Whatever India owns, the US must stop owning: no US on-call for transferred components, no US approval gate on India-owned changes, no US managers with India directs, and no "India builds, US approves" arrangement surviving past the first phase. Half-ownership is the one configuration every source in the research says fails.2324

The defense in full

Each drill-down is the argument the two-minute version compresses. Read them in any order.

Why not full domain ownership of commerce 1.2a

The domain-ownership success stories are real and they are all the wrong kind. Hotstar was built and scaled in India to 25 million and later 59 million concurrent viewers and its platform was reused for Disney+ in Southeast Asia, Israel, South Africa and the Middle East — but it was a home-market product with a local P&L, not a charter handed to a centre.1 documented Google's Bengaluru campus post says the same thing in its own words: "building from India, for the world," citing Google Pay.2 documented The pattern is India-first products that later globalized, not global products handed over. inferred

Commerce is also the domain with the densest set of US decision-makers per change — pricing, bundling, finance, legal, trust and safety — and the research on cross-site work is specific about what that costs: work items spanning sites took about 2.5 times as long, not because individuals were slower but because more people were needed to resolve each one, and time-zone separation hurt more than distance.1425 documented The site also lacks today the three things the documented domain owners hired first: a site head with a global title, product management, and second-line managers.1526 documented

And the Hotstar coda is the risk, not the reward: after losing the IPL rights in 2022, Hotstar "lost a considerable portion of its technology team" to the competitor, and Disney then folded the whole business into a joint venture it does not control.31 inferred A self-contained India organization is easy to lose whole.

Why not pure platform ownership 1.2b

This is the option the recommendation is closest to, and the difference matters in year three rather than year one. Platform charters are the modal successful centre: JPMorgan runs about 55,000 people in India on "technology solutions, business and functional support"; Warner Bros. Discovery describes its India hub as "fundamental to building our global technology platforms"; Lowe's India ended a ten-year graduation with India-based vice presidents owning applied AI and data platforms.41026 documented Platform work is coordinated by standardization, which is the kind of work distance tolerates.1713

But platform-only is also the modal drift. Citi's captive was sold to TCS after a decade as support; a 2024 practitioner survey describes captives that "become stagnant with marginal improvements over time" without external pressure; Sky cut 600 technology roles in 2025 while consolidating its platform operations.2756 documented Team Topologies' own framing is that a platform is a product with internal customers; a platform team with no customer on its own site loses that feedback loop.28 A platform-only site also builds the junior-heavy pyramid that never produces the second-line managers the site will need.29 judgment Adding one whole domain is what gives the site an outcome the US cannot ship without.

Why not a graduating extension 1.2c

The extension model is the lowest-risk option on paper and the documented stagnation path in practice. The market-wide picture: 56% of India centres are still execution shops, and about 27% reach "portfolio hub" ownership within five years — a figure from Zinnov, which sells centre set-ups, so treat it as an upper bound.7 documented Forrester found in 2007 that more than 60% of India captives were "struggling," and the 2008–09 divestiture wave was made of support charters that never got anything to own.3027 documented

The two celebrated graduations are slow and front-loaded: Target went from IT support in 2005 to an "integrated global headquarters" over about fifteen years; Lowe's from "one team, multiple locations" in 2015 to India-based platform VPs in ten — and both hired product, data science and managers in India before ownership moved.153126 documented

The structural problem is that during the extension phase ownership is diffuse by definition, and diffuse ownership is the best-replicated predictor of defects in the software-engineering literature — Microsoft's organizational metrics predicted failure-prone binaries at around 86% precision, beating code churn and complexity.23 documented Galbraith's point that rewards and processes decide whether a structure works cuts the same way: nothing in a US owner's incentives pushes a hand-off later.32 judgment The recommendation keeps the mechanism — dated gates, written criteria — and drops the starting state.

Why "whole components" rather than "the platform" 1.2d

Moving the entire platform on day one would strip the US of platform architecture while the site has no senior leadership — the vacuum Zinnov describes when "the first three hiring decisions shape the center more than the next ninety-seven combined" and centres run past 100 people with no permanent head.33 documented Team Topologies allows a platform to be several teams with clean interfaces, each a product for the stream-aligned teams it serves.34 Amazon's correction to the two-pizza rule was that team size was not the variable — dependencies were — and that every separable unit needs one accountable owner.24 documented So: three components with the cleanest interfaces (a schema, an SLA, an API) move first, each with one owner on one site; warehouse modeling, the semantic layer, governance and the ML platform stay in the US until a later, criteria-gated decision. judgment

Why sessions and QoE is the first domain 1.2e

The research tracks disagreed on this and the disagreement is instructive. The organization-design canon favours defence-heavy work — fraud, commerce data quality — because it is coordinated by standardized outputs.18 The India evidence favours telemetry-shaped domains — experimentation platform, QoE, sessions — because they have the fewest US business stakeholders.16 The case studies name QoE or sessions as the best-instrumented and least politically contested candidates, and name commerce packaging and funnels as the domains most exposed to strategy swings.2235 documented

The criterion all three actually share is: few US decision-makers per change, standardizable outputs, and separability from the warehouse's reciprocal dependencies. Sessions and QoE passes all three — telemetry in, metrics out, and its consumers are engineers and one analytics group, not the pricing committee. Fraud passes the first two but not cleanly the third. Commerce, funnels, browse and search fail the first while pricing and content strategy are set in the US. Experimentation splits: the platform is a capability, the analysis and review gate are not. judgment The worked scoring is on the Applying It page.

The phased path

Each phase names the ownership that transfers, the hires that must precede it, and the observable criteria that gate the next phase. A missed criterion holds the phase; it does not fail the plan. The sequence follows what the documented graduations did — leadership, product and managers in India before ownership moved — compressed from a decade to thirty months on the argument that the components transferring first are already worked on by the India contractors.1526 judgment

  1. Months 0–3

    Phase 0 — Foundation

    Nothing new transfers. Ownership decisions are announced with dates, not executed. The site head is hired first, with a global functional title — Head of Data Platform Engineering and Telemetry, say — reporting to the data executive directly; two-thirds of "mega" centre heads have technical backgrounds and many run a global portfolio in addition to the site.38 Vendor release terms are negotiated before any offer goes out, on the evidence that the provider will not "simply transfer all of their best people to you."5 Team APIs are written for every team on both sites, because remote work exposes poorly defined teams rather than causing them.39 Compensation bands are set at centre-market rates, 15–40% above IT-services bands at mid-senior levels.40

    Gate Site head in seat · at least two second-line managers hired or identified · conversion offers accepted by at least 60% of the contractors the organization actually wants, not the ones the vendor offers.

  2. Months 3–9

    Phase 1 — Three platform components

    Telemetry and event ingestion with its streaming pipelines; data quality, observability and contracts tooling; and the experimentation platform backend — assignment, exposure logging, metrics computation, not analysis and not the review gate. Each staffed at six or more in India, each with a named single-threaded owner, with runbooks, paved-road documentation and identical CI/CD and tooling on both sites — the conditions under which Microsoft found no quality penalty for distributed development.412413 The US stops owning these, including on-call.

    Gate Two consecutive quarters with change-failure rate and incident time-to-restore on transferred components within 30% of the pre-transfer baseline · India on-call at 100% for those components · fewer than 30% of India work items needing a US decision to close · converted-cohort attrition under 20% annualized.

  3. Months 9–18

    Phase 2 — The first domain: sessions and quality of experience

    End to end: pipelines, metric definitions, models, the analytics partner seat and the roadmap. An India product or product-analytics lead is hired before the transfer, because product management in India is what every documented domain owner had.1526 A decision-rights table — who decides metric changes, who is consulted — is signed by US playback and client engineering, using NBCUniversal's "embedded with and equally accountable to" phrasing for the US stakeholders.42 The US QoE team is dissolved into this; its people move to browse, search, funnels or commerce, or take the single US-side liaison seat.

    Gate QoE metrics authored in India adopted as the source of truth by US client and playback engineering · India-authored roadmap accepted in the annual planning cycle · median decision latency on India-owned changes needing US input at or below three business days · no US shadow team.

  4. Months 18–30

    Phase 3 — The second-domain decision

    Decided at month 18 against the phase-2 criteria, not sentiment. Either fraud and paid sharing as a data domain — pipelines, models, fraud metrics — with an India product lead, a US trust-and-safety and finance decision-rights table and a PII controls review; or, if the criteria are unmet, expansion of the platform charter into ML platform components and the catalog instead. Either outcome is a working site; only the undecided one is a failure. judgment

    Gate to steady state India site at 45–55 FTE with at most eight directs per manager43 · phase-2 criteria still holding · at least two India-based engineers promoted into staff or lead roles, as a proxy for a working pyramid.

What it does to the whole org

The India charter is a decision about the US organization as much as the India one. At steady state the ~140-person org is one hub-and-spoke: a central reporting line and hiring bar, people aligned to verticals, and the organization rather than the business partner prioritizing scarce talent — the Netflix shape, which at ten times this org's scale still keeps platform, metric definitions and experiment review central.4421 documented The site head sits on the data executive's staff as a peer of the US directors, not as a country manager; every India engineer reports to an India manager, because remote directs across a twelve-hour gap are the Herbsleb penalty applied to one-to-ones.14 judgment

Headcount, under the assumed baseline

About 40 contractor seats convert to 30–35 FTE at market bands, plus 10–15 net-new senior hires in India, landing at 45–50 India FTE and 90–95 US. The US does not shrink by fiat; it stops backfilling transferred seats. The baseline is assumed — see Applying It. judgment

What the US stops doing is listed, because it is the part that fails. US platform engineers stop owning, gatekeeping and carrying on-call for the three transferred components; they keep warehouse modeling, the semantic layer, governance, the ML platform, and platform architecture as a shared standard rather than a veto. The US sessions-and-QoE team ceases to exist as an owning team by month twelve. No US manager has India directs. And half-ownership is banned as a category: if a component cannot be owned whole on one site, it is not transferred.2324

Rewards and processes decide whether this works, not the chart — Galbraith's point that structure is the least powerful of the five levers.32 The India site gets its own hiring budget and a promotion cycle on the same calendar and ladder as the US; India-authored roadmaps enter the same planning process; on-call compensation is identical. Without those, the charter is a slide. judgment The full mapping — the three tests scored per domain, the ownership table, the org topology and the time-zone band — is on the Applying It page.

Tripwires — what would prove this wrong

Eight observable signals, each with what it means and the fallback. They are dated so that "not yet" has an expiry. The fallbacks are deliberately asymmetric: most of them retreat to a platform charter, because a platform site that works is better than a domain site that does not. judgment

  1. Tripwire · month 6

    No site head with a global functional title in seat. This is the leadership-vacuum failure mode; centres have run past 100 people with no permanent head.33

    Fallback

    Transfer nothing. Run as a managed extension with explicit Team APIs; revisit at month 9. An interim US leader is acceptable only on a written twelve-month posting with a named local successor.

  2. Tripwire · month 12

    Converted-cohort attrition above 25%, or offer acceptance below 50% of the people the organization wanted. Data and AI roles already run 18–25% attrition with 18–24-month tenures, and the vendor controls who is offered.455

    Fallback

    Pause phase 2. Re-band; renegotiate the vendor release; hire net-new rather than convert.

  3. Tripwire · any two quarters

    Change-failure rate or time-to-restore on a transferred component worse than baseline by more than 30% for two consecutive quarters. The evidence says this is team composition or tooling parity, not distance.13

    Fallback

    Re-pair with a US engineer in facilitating mode for one quarter; check the six-person floor; if still failing in the third quarter, transfer the component back and expand another.

  4. Tripwire · month 9

    More than 30% of India work items need a US decision to close. The boundary was drawn through a coupled system; ownership is diffuse in practice and the 2.5× applies.14

    Fallback

    Re-cut the component boundary; move the decision right to India or move the work back. Do not keep "owner" on a team that cannot close its own tickets.

  5. Tripwire · month 18

    QoE metrics authored in India are not adopted as source of truth by US engineering. The domain is not separable from its US stakeholders, or common ground was never built.46

    Fallback

    Return QoE to a US owner; expand India's platform charter instead. The site stays a platform site, and this memo's "one domain" claim was wrong for this organization.

  6. Tripwire · any time

    A US team re-creates capacity for a transferred component — "we can't wait for India." A decision-rights failure, not a capability failure; Amazon's lesson is that dependencies, not team size, predict failure.24

    Fallback

    Executive intervention within 30 days: dissolve the shadow team or formally re-transfer the component. Either is acceptable. Both at once is the failure.

  7. Tripwire · any time

    A P&L-driven reorg re-homes the data organization under a different executive — Disney moved its streaming data platform under the ad platform in April 2026 during a thousand-person cut.35

    Fallback

    The charter lives in Team APIs and ownership tables, not in a reporting line. Re-attach the site head to the new executive as a peer. Platform components are the most portable part of the org; they move with whoever owns data next.

  8. Tripwire · month 12

    Median decision latency on India-owned changes that need US input exceeds three business days. The time-zone interface is a stand-up, not a contract; temporal separation hurts more than spatial.2516

    Fallback

    Move the decision right to India for that class of change, or reclassify the work as shared and stop counting it as India-owned.

The strongest case for each rejected option

Each alternative gets its best argument first, then the reason it still loses.

Full domain ownership — give India commerce end to end 1.6a

Strongest case. Hotstar proves an India engineering organization can own a streaming product at record concurrency and export its platform to other markets.1 Amazon's lesson is to move the decision-maker with the team and to treat dependencies as defects; a whole domain with a single-threaded leader on site is exactly that.24 Contractors already touch commerce pipelines; commerce has the clearest P&L, so India would own an outcome the company cares about. Decisive ownership beats a five-year graduation that roughly three-quarters of sites never complete.7

Why it still loses. Hotstar and Google Pay were India-first products with local P&L; there is no verified case of a global consumer domain handed to a new centre on day one succeeding.2 Commerce has the densest US stakeholder set and the most latency-sensitive decisions; every pricing, bundling and paid-sharing decision becomes a cross-site work item at 2.5×.14 The site has no product manager, no second-line managers and no site head today — the three things the successful owners hired first.15 And a self-contained India organization is easy to lose whole.3 judgment

Pure platform ownership — India is the data platform, nothing else 1.6b

Strongest case. It is the modal successful centre charter — JPMorgan, WBD's hub, Lowe's platform VPs.41026 Platform work is coordinated by standardization, which distance tolerates; interfaces are APIs and schemas, not product debates.1713 It is what the contractors already do, fastest to charter, easiest to defend to a CFO.

Why it still loses as the whole charter. Captives with no product outcome depending on them stagnate and get sold or cut.2756 A platform with no internal customer on its own site loses the platform-as-product feedback loop.28 It builds a junior-heavy pyramid with no path to the second-line managers the site will need.29 The recommendation is mostly this option, with one domain added so the site has an outcome the US cannot ship without; the difference is small on paper and large in year three. judgment

Graduating extension — start as an extension of US teams, graduate on criteria 1.6c

Strongest case. Lowest immediate risk; it lets the organization learn who the converted people actually are before committing ownership. Target and Lowe's are real graduations.1531 Olson and Olson found that common ground must precede remote collaboration, and an extension phase is how common ground gets built.46 Galbraith's lateral-relations-first sequencing is respectable theory.47

Why it still loses. The base rate is about 27% in five years and the celebrated cases took ten to fifteen.7 During the extension phase ownership is diffuse — the defect predictor, and Spotify's "responsibility without accountability" pattern.2348 Nothing in the incentive structure pushes a US owner to hand off, and graduation criteria for centres are not documented anywhere the research could verify — the organization would be inventing them under pressure later. The recommendation keeps the mechanism and drops the starting state. judgment

Undifferentiated hybrid — decide per team, case by case 1.6d

Strongest case. Target's "center of gravity" rule is a hybrid and it worked; Walmart runs global platforms next to a separately owned local product company; domains differ, as the offence–defence framing of data strategy says they should.154918

Why it still loses. "Hybrid" is what mature sites became after a decade, not what they were chartered as.15 Without naming which domain and which components on day one, per-team choice produces the half-ownership configuration every source says fails, because each US owner chooses "extension" for their own team.2324 The recommendation is a hybrid with the choices made. judgment

What remains uncertain

  • The headcount baseline is assumed. If the India contractors are concentrated in commerce rather than platform and QoE, phase 1 becomes a change of hands as well as of ownership and takes longer. judgment
  • Everything about decision-rights frameworks, budget ownership and on-call at India centres is consultant material; the research found nothing rigorous. The decision-rights tables above are judgment.33
  • No source treats domain-versus-capability ownership at a remote site for data teams specifically; the recommendation extrapolates from software-engineering distance research and centre base rates.147
  • The 27% base rate and the attrition figures come from firms with a commercial interest in centres looking good.745
  • Attribution is weak everywhere. No company has published outcome data tying structure to results; this recommendation rests on avoiding documented failure modes more than on copying documented successes. judgment
