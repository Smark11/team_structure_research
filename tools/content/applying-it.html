Three tests, from the evidence

The five research tracks did not converge on a first domain. The canon argued for defence-heavy fraud and commerce data quality; the India evidence named QoE and sessions telemetry as a capability; the two case-study tracks named QoE or sessions as candidates for graduating domain ownership, with one of them listing fraud first.C3-42C3-45 What they shared, without saying so, was the criterion: how much does this work require people on the other site to say yes, and how often. The three tests below are that question decomposed, each traced to the numbered principles it comes from; a fourth consideration is a gate, not a test.

The multiplier2.5×

Calendar time for cross-site work items versus single-site ones at Lucent — because more people were needed per item, not because anyone was slower.C3-43

Test 1 — US decision-makers per change (principles 1, 4)

Cross-site work items took about 2.5× as long as single-site items at Lucent, and the mechanism was headcount per item, not slower people; temporal separation costs more than distance.C3-43C5-19 The test, applied to a unit of work: count the US people who must approve a typical change, in engineering as well as in the business. Commerce packaging needs pricing, finance, legal and trust-and-safety. Telemetry ingestion needs a schema owner. Quality of experience needs the engineering consumers of its metric definitions — playback, client, CDN and live operations, device certification — which is why it scores partial rather than pass, and why the score only holds because definition changes are rare and industry-standardized while pipeline and dimension changes need nobody.

Test 2 — Output standardizability (principles 1, 7)

Only one of Mintzberg's configurations — the divisionalized form, coordinated by standardization of outputs — is designed to run a unit at arm's length; adhocracy, coordinated by mutual adjustment, tolerates distance least, and DalleMule and Davenport's defensive-versus-offensive split maps the same distinction onto data work.C3-4C3-42 The test: can the unit's output be written down as a schema, an SLA or a metric definition that a consumer accepts without a conversation? Telemetry can. Fraud metrics can. A funnel analysis cannot.

Test 3 — Separability from the warehouse (principles 2, 5)

Warehouse facts are reciprocally interdependent — every domain reads every other domain's tables — so a boundary drawn through them produces silos before it produces modules, and the 2026 data-mesh retrospective found most organizations ended up with a thick central platform doing the domain work for exactly that reason.C3-52C3-33 The test: can the unit be owned whole without the warehouse's shared facts flowing through it? Ingestion sits upstream and passes. The session fact table is the most reciprocally-read table in a streaming warehouse — hours viewed, engagement, retention, content performance and finance's viewing numbers all derive from it — and fails; that is why sessionization stays central. QoE passes only with sessionization excluded: its metrics derive from playback events and its consumers read them, a sequential dependence that a contract can hold.

The legal gate — cross-border personal data

judgment The fourth consideration is not a test but a gate. Fraud and paid-sharing data means payment-instrument tokens, household graphs and, for EU subscribers, GDPR-scoped personal data processed in India under its own data-protection law; QoE telemetry means device and network identifiers but no payment instruments and no household graphs. Whether either is transferable as scoped cannot be known from a desk, which is why the legal review of telemetry, QoE and fraud data is a Phase 0 deliverable owned by privacy counsel — not a Phase 3 precondition — and why the matrix carries a question glyph wherever the answer waits on it.

The gate — one accountable owner, on one site (principles 3, 14)

A unit that passes all three tests is still not transferable unless one team on one site owns it whole — the Vista finding and Amazon's, from opposite ends.C3-8C1-31 If a unit cannot be owned whole on one site, it does not move.

Judgment — how the scoring resolves the disagreement

The tracks did not converge, and this page does not pretend they did. The canon voted for fraud; the India evidence named telemetry as a capability; both case-study tracks named QoE or sessions as graduating candidates, one with fraud ahead of them. The choice is this memo's, made against the three tests and the legal gate. Quality of experience wins on separability and on legal exposure, and it is the smallest outcome — playback SLOs, release gates, partner reporting; playback engineering could compute its own, and the Phase 2 gate tests whether it stops. Fraud wins on outcome relevance and loses on legal exposure and on decision latency. Sessionization stays central: the session fact table is the warehouse's spine, and a domain that includes it fails Test 3 outright, which is what was wrong with the earlier "sessions and QoE" framing. So QoE is the first domain, at month 9, on a written gate; fraud and ads measurement are the two candidates for the month-18 decision, and which one depends on the Phase 0 legal review and on whether the org has an ads tier. If India-authored QoE metrics are not the tables playback SLOs and release gates reference by month 18, the domain claim was wrong for this org and the site continues as a platform-plus-capability site (tripwire T5). That is lower risk first, and we say so.

Why not fraud first — the strongest alternative. Fraud is defence-heavy, output-standardized (chargeback rate, recovery revenue, false-positive rate), stable in definition, and coordinated by standardized outputs — Mintzberg's only configuration built to run at arm's length.C3-4C3-42 Its US stakeholders per change — trust and safety, finance, legal — are fewer than QoE's engineering consumers. And it is the outcome a CFO notices: paid-sharing enforcement drove the 2023–25 streaming P&L turns. It loses the first slot on two grounds. Personal data — payment instruments, household graphs, EU-scoped data processed in India — may make it non-transferable as scoped, and that is unknowable until the Phase 0 legal review. And its decisions are denominated in dollars, with the twelve-hour gap on every threshold change. Choosing QoE first is choosing lower risk over higher relevance; the memo does not pretend otherwise. Commerce packaging, conversion funnels, browse and search fail Test 1 for as long as pricing, bundling and content strategy are set in the US. Experimentation splits at a versioned contract: the engine is a capability with an API and moves; analysis, the catalog and the review gate are adhocratic, stakeholder-adjacent, and stay central — which is how every case study runs them.

What the US org stops doing

The distributed-ownership failures in the record share one feature: the sending site kept a hand on the work.C3-8C1-31 judgment So the transfer is written as a list of things the US organization ceases to do, with the principle behind each, because ownership that is added in India without being removed in the US is diffuse ownership under a new name.

The gap12h

Between Pacific and India at nominal hours; zero overlap without someone stretching (Fig. 4.3). Temporal separation costs coordination more than distance does.C5-19

  • US platform engineers stop owning, gatekeeping and carrying on-call for the transferred components — telemetry ingestion and the experimentation engine from month 3, data-quality and contracts tooling from month 9. They keep sessionization and the semantic layer, warehouse modeling, governance and access, the ML platform until Phase 3, the metrics catalog and its semantic definitions, and platform architecture as a shared standard, not a veto. The principle is Bird's: distributed components were not worse than collocated ones when ownership was clear and tooling shared — and the finding depends on the ownership being real.C3-9
  • The US QoE team is dissolved at the month-9 transfer. Its people move to browse, search, funnels or commerce, or take the one US-side liaison seat. The dissolution is announced at month 6, not month 0, and its members carry retention terms through month 12 — judgment because announcing a team's end nine months early is how the knowledge walks out before the transfer. Carmel and Agarwal's first tactic is to reduce intensive collaboration across sites; two teams owning one domain is the maximal-collaboration configuration.C3-45
  • No US manager has India directs. Every India engineer reports to an India manager; every India manager reports to the site head. judgment Remote directs across the twelve-hour gap are banned as a matter of position, not because a study measured them. The one stated exception is Phase 1: until the second-line managers are in seat, the site head runs the two Phase 1 teams directly with two tech leads, and the eight-direct rule is suspended for that period — stated here rather than hidden, because a site whose managers sit elsewhere is the leadership vacuum Zinnov describes.C5-9
  • Half-ownership is banned as a category after Phase 1. No "India builds, US approves" arrangement. The one deliberate exception is the versioned metric-definition contract in the experimentation engine, which is an X-as-a-service interface, not shared ownership; tripwire T4 tests whether it holds. If a component cannot be owned whole on one site it is not transferred — Nagappan's diffuse-ownership result and Amazon's dependencies-are-defects lesson say the same thing from opposite ends.C3-14C3-8C1-31
Where it breaks

Nothing in a US owner's incentives pushes the hand-off, and "we can't wait for India" is how shadow teams start. Tripwire T6 — the quarterly duplicate audit run by the metrics-catalog function — exists for exactly this.

judgment The US does not shrink by fiat. It stops backfilling the transferred seats — five to eight of them over three years — and the QoE team's people redeploy into the domains that stay, which is where the org is short anyway once four domains and the analysis function are explicitly US-anchored.

The team-size, span and cost arithmetic

Larson's rule is that a team is six to eight people, below four it is a project, and a manager should run three to five directs if hands-on and five to eight if people-focused, never more than eight.C3-23C3-24 McKinsey refuses a single number and gives five archetypes — player-coach 3–5, coach 6–7, supervisor 8–10, facilitator 11–15, coordinator 15+ — driven by how standardized the work is and how much of the manager's own time is individual contribution.C3-20 Platform work sits at the top of Larson's range, seven to eight; domain teams doing interlocked, uncertain work want five to seven. Nothing on either site runs at ten.

Put the floor and the ceiling together and the small-site problem appears. A twelve-person site asked to own two domains cannot honour a six-person team floor and an eight-direct span ceiling without a management layer nobody wants to fund; the frameworks are silent and budget decides.C3-23C3-24 Scaled to the whole org, the same arithmetic is why domain-owned data teams do not fit at 140: six people times eight domains is 48 heads of domain data engineering before any platform exists, which is the mesh retrospective's finding about why most organizations ended up with a thick central platform.C3-33

Reporting lines and rewards

The site head is a peer of the US directors on the data executive's staff, with a global functional title — Head of Data Platform Engineering and Telemetry, or whatever the components are actually called — and accountability for the transferred components and domains, not a country manager. The evidence favours a functional head hired first, because the first hires set the centre's ceiling, over either a country manager or a lift of the vendor's delivery leads; second-line managers are the scarce tier in the centre market, so the site head's first job is hiring them.C5-9C5-3 The search opens before this memo is signed, through a search firm plus internal candidates, at a director-plus band; if it runs past month 6 nothing transfers (tripwire T1), and a named India successor is a Phase 3 gate criterion so that the site is never one resignation from a vacuum.C5-6

Below the staff row the org keeps one shape on both sites: a central reporting line and hiring bar, people aligned to verticals, and the data org rather than the business partner deciding where scarce talent goes. That is Netflix's arrangement for a data organization an order of magnitude larger than this one, and it is the documented analogue for hub-and-spoke at any scale.C1-1 Experimentation analysis and the metrics catalog report centrally in the US, as they do at every case-study company that has written about it.C1-2C1-24

The QoE domain needs a second line of accountability written down, because its consumers — US playback and client engineering — will otherwise treat an India-owned domain as a vendor. NBCU's memo is the template: the research and data teams joined one central organization but "will remain embedded with and equally accountable to" the businesses they serve — the only explicit public phrasing of a dual-accountability data model in the case set.C2-33 Applied here, the India QoE team reports to the site head and is equally accountable to the US playback stakeholders named in its decision-rights table, and the adoption of its metrics as the tables behind playback SLOs and release gates is the Phase 2 gate.
